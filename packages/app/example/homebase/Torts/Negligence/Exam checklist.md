An issue-by-issue scaffold for a negligence problem question. For each issue: the rule, its authority, then apply it to the facts and conclude, before the next.

## 1. Duty of care

- **Rule**: a duty is owed where the case falls within a settled category, or the salient features support one.
- **Authority**: [[Donoghue v Stevenson]]; *Sullivan v Moody* (2001) 207 CLR 562. Notes: [[Duty of care]].
- [ ] Settled category? Name it.
- [ ] If not: foreseeability, control, vulnerability, coherence.

## 2. Breach

- **Rule**: was the risk foreseeable and not insignificant, and would a reasonable person have taken precautions? *Civil Liability Act 2002* (NSW) s 5B; *Wyong Shire Council v Shirt* (1980) 146 CLR 40.
- [ ] Probability and seriousness of harm, burden of precautions, social utility.

## 3. Causation

- **Rule**: factual causation and scope of liability. *Civil Liability Act 2002* (NSW) s 5D.
- [ ] Would the harm have happened anyway?

## 4. Remoteness

- **Rule**: was damage of this kind reasonably foreseeable? *Overseas Tankship (UK) Ltd v Morts Dock & Engineering Co Ltd (The Wagon Mound [No 1])* [1961] AC 388.

## 5. Defences

- [ ] Contributory negligence, voluntary assumption of risk, obvious risks.

## Conclusion

Answer the question asked, with the strongest arguments for it.
