The first element of negligence: did the defendant owe the plaintiff a duty to take reasonable care?

## Established categories

Many relationships are settled: manufacturer and consumer ([[Donoghue v Stevenson]]), driver and other road users, employer and employee, occupier and entrant. If the facts fall within one, say so, cite the authority and move on.

## Novel cases

Outside the settled categories, Australian courts do not apply a single test. They weigh the *salient features* of the relationship: foreseeability of harm, the defendant's control, the plaintiff's vulnerability, coherence with other areas of law, and any statute.[^1]

- **Reasonable foreseeability** of harm to a class including the plaintiff is necessary but not enough.
- **Vulnerability**: could the plaintiff protect themselves?
- **Coherence**: would a duty conflict with another legal duty or a statute?
